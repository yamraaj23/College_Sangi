Help Center - College Sangi Support

Last Updated: December 4, 2024

Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Visit the login page and click on "Sign up here". Fill in your email, password, university, and course information. You'll receive a confirmation email to activate your account.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot password?" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the instructions sent to your email to reset your password.

Using the Forum

How do I create a new post?

Navigate to the Forum section and click "New Post". Write your question or discussion topic, select your course, and click "Post".

How do I reply to a post?

Click on a post to open it, scroll to the comments section, type your reply, and click "Comment".

Can I edit or delete my posts?

Yes, you can edit or delete your own posts. Click the menu icon on your post and select the desired action.

Marketplace

How do I list a textbook for sale?

Go to the Marketplace section and click "Sell Your Textbook". Fill in the book title, author, condition, price, and add photos. Click "List for Sale".

How do I buy a textbook?

Browse the marketplace, select a book, and click "Buy". You'll be directed to complete the transaction securely.

Is the marketplace safe?

Yes, we encourage buyers and sellers to meet in public, safe locations. Always verify product condition before completing transactions.

Events

How do I create an event?

Click "Add Event" in the Events section. Fill in event details (title, date, time, location, description) and click "Create Event".

How do I register for an event?

Click on an event to view details, then click "Register" or "Going" to indicate your attendance.

Question Bank

How do I upload question papers?

Click "Upload Question Photo" in the Question Bank section. Select your course, year, and semester. Choose image files and click "Upload".

Can I download question papers?

Yes, click the download button on any question paper to save it to your device.

Profile & Account

How do I update my profile?

Click on "My Profile" and then "Edit Profile". Modify your information and click "Save Changes".

How do I change my password?

Go to your profile settings and select "Account Settings". Find the password field and enter your new password.

How do I delete my account?

In your profile, click "Delete Profile" and follow the confirmation process. Be aware that this action is permanent.

Privacy & Security

Is my information secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I make my profile private?

You can adjust privacy settings in your account settings to control who sees your profile information.

What should I do if I see suspicious activity?

Report it immediately to our support team at support@collegesangi.net or through the report function on the site.

Troubleshooting

I can't log in to my account

Try resetting your password using the "Forgot password?" link. Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again. If the problem persists, contact support.

The website is loading slowly

Check your internet connection, clear browser cache, or try using a different browser. Contact support if issues continue.

I'm not receiving emails from College Sangi

Check your spam folder. Update your email address in account settings. Verify your notification preferences are enabled.
