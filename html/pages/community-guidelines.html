Community Guidelines for College Sangi

Last Updated: December 4, 2024

1. Welcome to College Sangi Community

College Sangi is a vibrant, inclusive platform designed to foster academic collaboration and meaningful connections among university students. These Community Guidelines help maintain a safe and respectful environment.

2. Be Respectful and Inclusive

  • Treat others with kindness and respect
  • Avoid discrimination and harassment
  • Celebrate diversity and different viewpoints
  • Use inclusive and respectful language
  • Support each other in learning and growth

3. Maintain Academic Integrity

  • Share only your own original work
  • Provide proper attribution to sources
  • Do not engage in plagiarism or cheating
  • Respect intellectual property rights
  • Engage in genuine academic discussions

4. Content Standards

  • No illegal or hate speech content
  • No explicit sexual or graphic content
  • No spam or promotional content
  • No harassment or threats
  • Respect others' privacy
  • Do not spread misinformation

5. Forum Etiquette

  • Stay on topic in discussions
  • Search before posting questions
  • Be constructive in your feedback
  • Write clearly with proper grammar
  • Avoid using ALL CAPS
  • No suspicious links or spam

6. Reporting Violations

Help us maintain a safe community:

  • Use the report function on posts and profiles
  • Email us at community@collegesangi.net
  • Contact campus security for urgent concerns
  • Reports are handled confidentially

7. Consequences of Violation

  • Warning for minor first-time violations
  • Content removal for violations
  • Temporary suspension for repeated violations
  • Permanent ban for severe violations
  • Possible notification to your institution

8. Appeals

If you believe action was taken in error, you can appeal within 30 days. Provide evidence supporting your appeal to our review team.
